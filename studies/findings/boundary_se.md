## 0.0.7.9000

Run on 2026-09-23, R 4.4.3.

Standard errors of the fixed effects when a random-effect covariance sits at its boundary, under three rules computed at the same optimum:
A holds the whole boundary term; B is the fit's own, the rule the package
used at this version; C3 holds only the directions with curvature below
1e-3 of the term's largest. Coverage of 95% intervals among the fits at a
boundary; se_sd is mean SE over the SD of the estimates across all usable fits.

1433 of 2800 fits ended at a boundary, in the 7 of 10 regimes where any did. C3's coverage is at least as close to 0.95 as A's in 7 of those 7; its lowest is 0.893 (rare).
Against C3, A's standard errors run a median 0.982 to 0.997 times as large by regime, and as little as 0.547 in the worst fit.
B, the rule in use at this version, runs 0.547 to 1.340 times C3's.
Where the reduced model could be refitted (630 fits), C3 matched it to a ratio of 0.979 to 1.014.
Paired, C3 covered 33 intervals that A missed, and A covered 0 that C3 missed.
Every boundary fit had a gap of at least 2.3 orders of magnitude between its flat and curved directions; thresholds of 1e-3 and 1e-4 chose differently in 49 of 1433 fits.

CAVEAT that must travel with this result: a regime can under-cover under
every rule, and when it does the cause is not the choice of standard
error -- in `combined` it is the rare category's bias. AR(1) and CAR(1)
terms are held the same way but are not part of this study.
