## model
A multinomial model of m3, fitted to 240 observations. The model works on the logit scale, so its coefficients are not in percentage points of probability; the effects below are converted.

## effects
x: very strong evidence (p < 0.001) that x is associated with m3. Across the middle half of x, from -0.871 to 0.678, the predicted share of 'a' is 52.5% against 14.9%, of 'b' 42.9% against 71.3% and of 'c' 4.64% against 13.8%.

## caveats
These are associations. Nothing here rules out a common cause of a predictor and the response, so an effect could differ in size or sign from what is reported. To say more you need a design that identifies one: ilm_dag_model() with an adjustment set, ilm_did(), ilm_rdd().
