## model
A poisson model of kt, fitted to 240 observations. The model works on the log scale, so its coefficients are not in counts; the effects below are converted.

## effects
x: very strong evidence (p < 0.001) that x is associated with kt. Across the middle half of x, predicted kt is 0.99 at -0.871 against 1.58 at 0.678: 0.594 higher (95% interval 0.451 to 0.738). (Rate ratio 1.36 per unit of x.)

## diagnostics
All 6 fitting checks passed: the optimiser converged, the gradient is at zero and the information matrix is usable. These say the fit is sound, not that the model is right -- for that, run ilm_appraise().

## caveats
These are associations. Nothing here rules out a common cause of a predictor and the response, so an effect could differ in size or sign from what is reported. To say more you need a design that identifies one: ilm_dag_model() with an adjustment set, ilm_did(), ilm_rdd().
The model has an offset, so the effects on the mean are per unit of exposure: the offset, offset(log(t)), at 0 (t = 1 for a log offset); `per =` sets another.
